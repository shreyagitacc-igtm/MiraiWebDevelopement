My Favorite Places

Here is a collection of my top three favorite travel destinations around the world. Each place holds a unique charm, stunning landscapes, and unforgettable experiences.

Quick Jump

  1. Paris
  2. Tokyo
  3. New York

Paris

Things to do

  • Visit the Eiffel Tower at sunset
  • Explore the art masterpieces inside the Louvre Museum
  • Stroll down the historic Champs-Élysées avenue

Learn more about this city on the Paris Wikipedia page.

Tokyo

Things to do

  • Experience the famous Shibuya Crossing
  • Visit the historic Senso-ji Temple
  • Enjoy fresh sushi at the outer Tsukiji Fish Market

Learn more about this city on the Tokyo Wikipedia page.

New York

Things to do

  • Walk across the historic Brooklyn Bridge
  • Relax or rent a boat in Central Park
  • Watch a spectacular Broadway musical theater production
